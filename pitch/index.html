SoundGuide

Hear the room
3D sound that warns you before you walk into something.

Picture walking into a room you've never seen, with your eyes closed. For 43 million blind people, that's every day. We built SoundGuide.
the problem
43M blind worldwide
  • The canefinds the floor, misses the table edge at hip height or the half-open door.
  • Guide dogscost tens of thousands to train and have long waitlists.
  • Indoor navneeds beacons installed first, or a LiDAR iPhone Pro.
A cane finds what's at your feet. It misses the table edge at hip height or the door left half open. Guide dogs cost tens of thousands and have long waitlists. And most indoor navigation needs beacons installed first, or an expensive LiDAR phone.
the fix

Your iPhone becomes a sonar

  • Beeps speed upas the way ahead closes in, like a parking sensor.
  • Buzz from the real directionSpatial audio puts the warning where the obstacle actually is.
  • Zero setupAny recent iPhone + headphones. No LiDAR, no beacons, no internet.
SoundGuide turns any recent iPhone and a pair of headphones into a sonar. Beeps get faster as the way ahead closes in, and anything you're about to walk into buzzes from its real direction in 3D. No setup, no LiDAR, no internet.
how it works

All on-device

01 MapARKit planes + feature points build the room as you walk.
02 ScanAn 80 cm corridor, 2 m ahead, checked 10× a second.
03 NameYOLO26n on the Neural Engine labels what's there.
04 PlaceHRTF spatial audio + haptics, by time to impact.
05 AskHold the screen: Apple's on-device model describes the view.
Under the hood: ARKit maps planes and feature points as you walk. We check an 80-centimetre corridor two metres ahead, ten times a second. YOLO on the Neural Engine names what's there. HRTF spatial audio and haptics place each warning where the object is, timed by time to impact. And if you want more, hold the screen and ask Apple's on-device model what the camera sees.
